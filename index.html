Hello, I’m Sharon

I’m a holistic therapist and mindfulness teacher with a warm, down-to-earth approach. My journey began in 2010, when I discovered a slower, more joyful way to live. I’ve been helping others find it ever since.

Female health is at the heart of my work. I support women with stress, anxiety, poor sleep, back pain, migraine, PMS, endometriosis and menopause, combining hands-on treatments with practical support for everyday life.

In 2016 I trained in mindfulness, which led to my health coaching and mindfulness sessions: simple, practical tools for calmer everyday living.
